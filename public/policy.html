Kindgleam

Terms and usage policy

Kindgleam is an AI assistant for learning, work, business ideas, code and research. It helps with anything good and declines what would hurt people. This page says, in plain words, what it will and won't do.

Who can use it

You must be 16 or older (or the age your operator sets). You confirm this and accept these terms the first time you use Kindgleam, and again when they change.

What it helps with

  • Learning: explanations at your level, step-by-step help that teaches rather than just gives answers, practice questions and study plans.
  • Business ideas: customers, markets and competitors with sources, pricing, business models and projections, clearly labelled as estimates.
  • Code: writing, explaining, reviewing, debugging and testing code, run in a sealed sandbox with no internet.
  • Everything else good: writing, research with sources, reading your files, calculations and reminders.

Topics that need care

For these, Kindgleam helps with general information, says what it doesn't know, and tells you when to see a professional:

  • Health: general information, not a diagnosis. In an emergency it tells you to call your local emergency number first.
  • Law: general information, not legal advice. Rules depend on your country.
  • Money: general information, not financial advice. Projections are estimates.
  • Decisions about people (hiring, grades, loans, benefits): it can help organise information, but a person must decide.
  • Hard moments: if you are thinking about harming yourself, it answers with care and points you to people who can help right away.

What it declines

It won't help anyone do these things. It will still explain them, help you protect yourself, and help you report or recover from them.

  • Anything that sexualises or endangers children.
  • Sexual or intimate images of real people without their consent, and fake images meant to deceive or humiliate someone.
  • Identifying people from their face, or guessing someone's ethnicity, religion, sexuality, politics or health from how they look.
  • Weapons or methods that could seriously hurt many people, and help carrying out violence.
  • Harmful software, or breaking into accounts, devices or systems that aren't yours.
  • Scams, phishing, forged documents, fake reviews and other deception for gain.
  • Threats, harassment, hate against people for who they are, and publishing someone's private details.
  • Tracking, monitoring or secretly watching another person.
  • Making or getting illegal drugs.
  • Misleading voters or interfering with elections.
  • Pretending to be a real person or organisation to deceive others.
  • Scoring people socially, reading people's emotions at work or school, or manipulating people who are vulnerable.

When it declines, it doesn't just say no. It tries to understand what you really need, says briefly what it can't help with, and helps with that need in a safe way, in your language. For its safety records, only the kind of request is kept, never what you wrote; the chat itself is yours to keep or delete. If someone keeps asking for these things, new chats pause for a while.

Topics not discussed

Religion. Kindgleam does not discuss religious topics, for any religion, equally. This covers beliefs, practices, rulings, scripture and its meaning, religious figures, comparing religions, religious debates and religious history. For religious questions, please ask a qualified scholar or leader of your faith.

When a request only mentions religion in passing (for example planning your week around a holiday), it helps with the rest and stays neutral. It never keeps your religion or beliefs in memory, and its answers never state religious teachings or rulings.

Images

  • It describes images you share and reads the text in them. It does not identify people from their faces or guess sensitive traits from how they look.
  • It never makes sexual images of real people. Charts and pictures it makes with code follow the same rules.

Situation-aware governance

Every request is evaluated in context. The system considers the situation's risk, data sensitivity, jurisdiction, human decision needs, external tools and side effects before it chooses how much assistance, review or approval is needed.

High-impact or regulated work can require the applicable country or region, current evidence and human review. The system does not treat AI output as legal, medical, financial, safety or regulatory certification.

Privacy is part of the same decision: data is minimized, user and workspace boundaries are preserved, and private data is not sent to the Gemini API or another external destination without the required consent and authorization.

Honesty

  • It is an AI and can make mistakes. Check important information.
  • It cites sources for facts it looked up, and says when it is unsure.
  • It never says it did something it didn't do. It asks before anything that changes things for you, such as adding a tool or a reminder.

Your data

  • Your messages go to the Google Gemini API only so it can answer, and only after the required consent is given. Payments go through Stripe; card numbers are never seen or kept here.
  • Memory: Kindgleam remembers lasting things you tell it (like your work or how you like answers) so later chats don't start from zero. Only you can see your memories. You can view, delete or turn memory off in Settings → Personalization.
  • You can delete any of your chats and files, and your memory, at any time.
  • It doesn't connect to your email, cloud drives or bank.

Reporting a problem

Use the flag under any answer to report it as harmful, wrong, unfair or a privacy problem. Your workspace's administrators review reports.

Terms of use

  1. Use Kindgleam lawfully and within this usage policy. You are responsible for how you use what it produces.
  2. Don't try to get around its safeguards, or use it to harm, deceive or spy on people.
  3. Answers may be wrong or incomplete and are not professional advice. Check anything important before relying on it.
  4. Your workspace's administrators may review reported answers and the kinds of requests that were declined.
  5. Accounts that break these terms may be paused or closed.
  6. These terms may change. You will be asked to accept a new version before you continue.
