# Kindgleam brand

## The one logo image

**`public/kindgleam.png`** is the only logo image in the system. It is a 1024 × 1024 PNG with a transparent background.

The same file is used everywhere:

- the browser tab icon;
- the iPhone home-screen icon;
- the installed-app icon (the manifest);
- the header and sign-in badges;
- the assistant's chat avatar.

There are no per-place variants. To change the logo, replace this one file.

## The mark

The mark is two concentric rings on one centre, in indigo `#4F46E5`.

- The outer ring has two openings at opposite corners. Each opening holds one dot.
- The inner ring is a full circle whose radius is the outer radius ÷ φ (golden ratio).

This indigo was chosen because it stays visible on both light and dark backgrounds, so one file works in light mode, dark mode, and on the black backing iOS puts behind transparent icons.

## Vector sources (for print and design tools, not used by the app)

- `svg/kindgleam-mark.svg`, `pdf/kindgleam-mark.pdf`: the mark.
- `svg/kindgleam-logo.svg`, `pdf/kindgleam-logo.pdf`: the mark with the "kindgleam" wordmark (Inter SemiBold, outlined, tracking −1.5%).
- `geometry.json`: the exact construction numbers.

## Rules

- Do not recolour parts of the mark separately, add effects, or change its proportions.
- Keep clear space of at least one dot's width around it.
